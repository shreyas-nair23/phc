# **Project Proposal: Autonomous PHC Medicine Replenishment ****&**** Supply Chain Resilience Engine**
 
## **1. Problem Statement ****&**** Context**
 
Public healthcare across rural India faces severe medicine supply chain vulnerabilities. Primary Health Centres (PHCs) frequently suffer from stock-outs of essential medicines (e.g., Paracetamol, ORS, antibiotics) and wastage due to expired drugs. The root causes include:
 
- **High Data-Entry Friction:** Overburdened PHC staff rely on manual paper registers, causing delayed stock updates.
 
- **Reactive Replenishment:** Reorders are placed only after stock hits zero, ignoring vendor delivery lead times.
 
- **Communication Gaps:** Local vendors and district warehouses operate in regional languages and lack integration with rigid central dashboards.
 
## **2. Core Idea ****&**** Solution Overview**
 
An **AI-Powered Autonomous Supply Replenishment ****&**** Inter-PHC Redistribution Platform** designed to ensure zero medicine stock-outs across rural healthcare networks.
 
Instead of acting as a passive inventory dashboard, the system automates the end-to-end replenishment lifecycle:
 
- **Zero-Touch Ingestion:** Captures stock via invoice/package scanning using Gemini Vision.
 
- **Predictive Reordering:** Automatically detects low stock and upcoming expiries based on daily consumption velocity.
 
- **Automated Vernacular Dispatch:** Generates and dispatches reorder requests to local vendors in their native language via WhatsApp/SMS.
 
- **End-to-End Tracking ****&**** Inter-PHC Transfers:** Tracks incoming shipments in real time and auto-routes emergency transfers from nearby surplus PHCs if vendor delivery is delayed.

### **Layer 1: Data Capture ****&**** Ingestion (Frontend + Gemini Vision)**
 
- **Offline-First PWA/Android App:** Allows logging of medicine stock, batch numbers, and expiry dates even with zero internet connectivity.
 
- **Gemini Vision OCR:** Staff snap a photo of a paper delivery register, stock invoice, or medicine carton. Gemini parses the image into structured JSON (drug_name, batch_no, quantity, expiry_date) to eliminate manual typing errors.
 
- **Vernacular Voice Logging:** Staff can log daily distribution via voice notes in regional languages (e.g., Hindi, Kannada, Tamil).
 
### **Layer 2: Intelligence ****&**** Demand Engine (Backend Core)**
 
- **Dynamic Reorder Calculation:** Reorders trigger based on consumption velocity and supplier lead times rather than static zero-stock numbers:
 
Requisition Sent → Vendor Confirmed → In Transit → Delivered & Verified.
 
- **Expiry Watchlist Engine:** Scans inventory daily for batches expiring within 30–60 days and flags them for priority distribution or transfer.
 
- **Inter-PHC Redistribution Logic:** If a primary vendor lead time exceeds remaining stock days, the system identifies neighboring PHCs within a 20 km radius with excess inventory and generates an emergency peer-to-peer transfer request.
 
### **Layer 3: Action ****&**** Delivery Tracking (Communications ****&**** Workflow)**
 
- **Multilingual Vendor Requisition:** Uses Gemini API to format verified database orders into localized text/audio messages dispatched via WhatsApp/SMS.
 
- **Live Pipeline Tracking:** Monitors order lifecycles through four distinct states: Requisition Sent $\rightarrow$Vendor Confirmed $\rightarrow$ In Transit $\rightarrow$ Delivered & Verified.

## **6. Project Evaluation: Pros, Cons ****&**** Mitigations**
 
### **Positive Sides (Strengths ****&**** Differentiators)**
 
- **Solves Real Public Health Need:** Directly target e-Aushadhi bottlenecks and rural drug stock-outs across Indian states.
 
- **Eliminates Manual Data Friction:** Vision-based scanning removes the primary operational barrier for overworked PHC nurses and pharmacists.
 
- **Action-Oriented Architecture:** Moves beyond passive visualization by taking active steps (generating reorders and executing stock transfers).
 
- **High Scalability:** Designed for multi-tier healthcare structures (PHC $\rightarrow$ Community Health Centre $\rightarrow$ District Warehouse).
 
### **Negative Sides (Risks ****&**** Potential Pitfalls)**
 
- **LLM Hallucination Risk:** Generative AI misinterpreting drug names or quantities could lead to incorrect orders.
 
- **Vendor Digital Exclusion:** Local Tier-3 vendors may ignore automated messages or fail to update delivery statuses.
 
- **Intermittent Connectivity:** Deep rural PHCs often lack stable 4G/5G connections needed for real-time API calls.
 
### **Risk Mitigations**
 
- **Deterministic Guardrails:** Raw calculations and drug IDs are managed strictly by backend code. Gemini is restricted to text translation and invoice parsing using strict JSON schema validation.
 
- **Human-in-the-Loop Approval:** Reorders require a single confirmation tap from the PHC pharmacist before sending.
 
- **Store-and-Forward Architecture:** Local actions queue in IndexedDB while offline and execute automatic background syncs once connectivity is restored.
